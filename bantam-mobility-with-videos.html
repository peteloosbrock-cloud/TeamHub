Hip & Ankle Mobility

Build the movement quality needed for deeper knee bend, better skating positions, and stronger lateral pushes.

15 minutesAges 14–153–4 times per weekNo equipment

Before you start

Warm up with 2–3 minutes of marching, jogging in place, or jumping jacks. Move slowly and stay in a comfortable range. Stop any movement that causes sharp pain or pinching.

Workout progress: 0 of 7

Tap “Watch YouTube Example” for each movement. The first button opens a specific demonstration; the others open YouTube results for that exercise so you can choose a demonstration.

Four-week challenge

Complete this routine 3–4 times each week. Check off each week after meeting your goal.

Coach's reminders

Quality over speed. Don't bounce or force stretches. Keep your heel down during ankle drills, and control your knees during squats. Consistent mobility practice supports skating technique, but doesn't replace on-ice training.
